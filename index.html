About Ryan

Born in Bakersfield, California, Ryan Stupak was raised in a household filled with colorful personalities, strong emotions, and stories worth telling. The experiences of his childhood, both the good and the difficult, helped shape the perspective, empathy, and emotional depth he would later bring to his performances. Growing up, Ryan found an appreciation for the power of storytelling and the ability of a voice to inspire laughter, create connection, and transport audiences to entirely different worlds. Those early influences would eventually lead him into voice acting, where he discovered a passion for bringing characters and stories to life. Today, Ryan works as a professional voice actor, audiobook narrator, voice coach, and content creator under RedReaperStudios. With multiple published audiobooks, character performances, and independent productions to his credit, he continues to build a career centered around immersive storytelling, memorable characters, and authentic performances.

About My Setup

Ryan operates from a custom-built recording studio that he designed and constructed himself with a focus on professional-quality sound isolation and acoustic treatment. The approximately 8' x 8' booth features heavily reinforced walls built with multiple layers of drywall and extensive Rockwool insulation to minimize outside noise. The floor and ceiling are independently treated as part of a comprehensive isolation design, while additional exterior treatment helps reduce unwanted sound transmission into the recording space. Inside the booth, recording quality is further enhanced through a two-stage acoustic treatment system. The first layer consists of thick acoustic foam panels arranged throughout the space, complemented by bass traps in the corners to help control low-frequency buildup. A secondary internal frame supports heavy moving blankets that create an additional layer of absorption, helping produce a clean, controlled recording environment with minimal reflections. Ryan records using a Rode NT1 microphone paired with a Focusrite Scarlett Solo (4th Generation) audio interface. His production workflow is built around Audacity, with additional attention paid to reducing reflective surfaces within the recording space to ensure the cleanest possible audio capture. The result is a recording environment specifically designed to deliver clear, professional voiceover audio suitable for audiobooks, character performances, commercials, narration, and online content. Every recording is produced from a dedicated studio environment designed to meet the technical requirements of modern voiceover production.
